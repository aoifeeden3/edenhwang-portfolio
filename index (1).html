Give Orange

Give Orange is a student founded nonprofit that counts donors rather than dollars, so the goal was never one large gift but many small ones. What I do

How a brief becomes a live campaign, and what I own at each stage.

  • Plan against the brief

    Turn a launch date, an event, or a campaign goal into a calendar where every post has a job and an audience.

  • Make the work

    Shoot the stills, film and cut the short form video, then design the layouts and carousels.

  • Write for the audience

    Match the voice to who is reading. At Porsche that meant writing every post twice, natively in Korean and English, rather than translating one into the other.

  • Ship it, then read it

    Route drafts through brand and legal review, publish on schedule, then look at what landed and carry it into the next round.

Design notes

Why a marketing portfolio looks like a candy box.

This site is built from my favorite candy. Meiji Apollo comes in a red box covered in strawberries, with a gold burst on the corner asking whether you got the lucky star inside. I took its grammar, not its picture.

  • Color, straight off the carton. Red acts, cream holds everything, cocoa brown stands in for black, and one gold is saved for a single reward.
  • Heavy line, flat fill. No gradients, no soft shadows. Shadows are hard offsets, like a print run out of register.
  • Borrowed shape. The die-cut scallop on the box lid became the hem of the navigation bar.
  • Extended, not copied. The box only ever had to draw strawberries. A portfolio needs a bee, a football, a heart, a camcorder, and a sprout, so I drew those to its rules instead of borrowing them.
  • Candy red
  • Blush
  • Milk cream
  • Cocoa ink
  • Lucky gold
  • Streak. Lucky stars shoot across the header on their own timing, trails and all.
  • Float. Berries, cones, stars, and a bee drift on their own loops and shift with the page.
  • Bounce. Hover a post row and its icon hops, the row lifts, and a hard shadow snaps out.
  • Burst. Click the button up top and stars scatter. A pink one means you got the lucky star.
