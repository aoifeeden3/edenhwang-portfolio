About the design

This palette comes from looking at objects at rest: the particular green of a sage leaf, the color of old terracotta, a piece of cream paper left in the sun. Nothing here is fully saturated — every color has been mixed with a little white, the way a still life painting holds its light.

Serif headlines set in italic carry the warmth; a lighter sans underneath keeps the reading calm. The layout leaves room around everything, because the work itself already has color and doesn't need help competing for attention.

Motion here is quiet on purpose, but not still. A soft light follows the cursor through the header. Numbers lift slightly when you reach them. Titles draw an underline as you hover a piece of work, the way a pencil might trace under a line worth reading twice. The page tracks how far you've read with a thin mark that travels as you scroll.

Small gestures, meant to reward attention rather than demand it.
